\section{Introduction}
This report presents the comprehensive analysis of Caspian Sea level variations
from 1992 to 2025 and a forecast for 2026-2030 based on a weighted linear regression model.
\section{Methodology}
A weighted linear regression model was applied to the annual mean sea level data
from 2016 to 2025. The model is defined as:
\begin{equation}
\text{Sea Level} = a \cdot \text{Year} + b
\end{equation}
where $a$ is the trend (slope) and $b$ is the intercept.
